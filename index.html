How it works

Relative humidity changes with temperature, so LuftM compares dew points, which measure the actual water vapour. If outdoor air’s dew point is at least 2 °C below your indoor dew point, swapping some air can dry the room. Heat figures count only the air you replace, using Q = 1.2 × V × 1005 × ΔT. Warmed-air humidity is a theoretical estimate, not a prediction of your room. Condensation is judged only from a surface temperature you measure.

About

Advice is indicative. It cannot guarantee that condensation or mould is prevented, and is no substitute for heating, proper ventilation, or fixing leaks and rising damp. You decide when to open windows.
